Moonwatcher

Install the firmware on your board, straight from the browser.

  1. Connect the board to this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Choose your board below and click “Install”. Your browser asks which port to use – pick the board.
  3. After installing, connect your phone to the Wi-Fi Moonwatcher-Setup and enter your home Wi-Fi.
This browser can't flash devices. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure connection (https).

A fresh install erases the board, including saved Wi-Fi and settings. Updates later arrive over Wi-Fi by themselves.
