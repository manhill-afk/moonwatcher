Moonwatcher

Install the firmware on your board, straight from the browser.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux). Phones, Firefox and Safari can't install.
  2. Connect the board to this computer with a USB-C data cable. (Some cables only charge – if no port shows up in step 4, try another cable.)
  3. Choose your board below and click “Install”.
  4. A window lists the ports. Pick the board – usually called “USB JTAG/serial debug unit” or “USB Serial Device (COM…)” – and click “Connect”.
  5. Tick “Erase device” for a clean start, click “Install” and wait 1–2 minutes until it says “Installation complete”. The eye starts by itself.
  6. Wi-Fi: on your phone, join the Wi-Fi Moonwatcher-Setup. A page opens (if not, open 192.168.4.1 in the browser). Tap “Configure WiFi”, pick your home Wi-Fi, enter the password, Save.
    Good to know: only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi works (if your network is missing, use the one without “5G”). If your phone asks “No internet – stay connected?”, say yes.
  7. After about a minute the eye shows the market. Settings: on the eye, swipe up → Setup → hold, then scan the QR code with your phone.
This browser can't flash devices. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Flashing needs a secure connection (https).

A fresh install erases the board, including saved Wi-Fi and settings. Updates later arrive over Wi-Fi by themselves.
