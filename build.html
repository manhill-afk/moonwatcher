Moonwatcher

Build your own Moonwatcher in about 15 minutes: buy one board with a metal case, install the firmware from your browser, plug in USB-C – done. No soldering, no 3D printer, no coding.

You end up with a round eye on your desk that shows the crypto market: colour = trend, pupil = volatility, gaze = trading volume, sparkle = distance from the high. The firmware is free and updates itself over Wi-Fi.

Can't or don't want to build it yourself? Drop me a line at moonwatcher@funktronix.de and we'll find a way.

1. Buy the board

You need exactly one part: the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28-B – the version with the metal case. Shops often call it “ESP32-S3 Development Board with 1.28inch Round Touch LCD, Accelerometer Gyroscope Sensor”.

Buy it e.g. at Eckstein (Germany, exactly this version with metal case), from the official Waveshare shop or a trusted electronics store – avoid marketplace listings, they are often mislabelled.

Watch out for look-alikes – these look almost the same but won't work:

BoardProblem
RP2040-LCD-1.28no Wi-Fi – doesn't work at all
ESP32-S3-LCD-1.28 (without “Touch”)no tapping, no menu
cheap ESP32-C3 round boardstoo little memory, no motion sensor

The metal case weakens Wi-Fi a little: don't put the eye too far from your router.

2. What else you need

No tools – just things most homes already have:

  • a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) with Chrome or Edge – Firefox only in a roundabout way, phones and Safari can't install
  • a USB-C data cable (some cables only charge)
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi with internet (5 GHz networks don't work)
  • a phone to set up Wi-Fi
  • later, a normal USB power adapter, e.g. a phone charger

3. Assembly

There's nothing to wire: the -B board comes ready-assembled in its metal case, with the USB-C socket reachable from outside.

  1. Unpack the board, plug in the USB-C data cable, other end into your computer.
  2. The board starts as soon as it gets power – there's no power button. Whatever it shows now (a maker demo) doesn't matter; it gets replaced in a moment.

Got a 3D printer? Print the Moonwatcher Lander – a stand made for the eye in its metal case, so it stands even better on your desk: download (3MF). Print in PLA or PETG with supports. Feed the USB-C cable up through the stand from below, plug it into the eye and set the eye in.

For tinkerers: a 3D-printable eye-shaped case is on its way – it uses the board without metal case and needs a little soldering.

4. Install and set up

The install page walks you through the rest step by step: put the firmware on the board straight from the browser, then connect the eye to your Wi-Fi with your phone. Takes about 10 minutes.

Open the install page

After that, nothing to do: updates arrive over Wi-Fi by themselves.
